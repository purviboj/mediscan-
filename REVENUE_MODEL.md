# Medi-Scan Revenue Model (Aligned to Slides)

## Revenue Architecture

1. Hardware License (entry fee, one-time capital sale)
- Camera + compute unit per OR suite
- Price: `$18,000-$25,000` per unit

2. SaaS Platform Subscription (recurring)
- Annual software license for model updates, cloud sync, compliance tools
- Price: `$8,400` per suite per year

3. Clinical Training and Onboarding (adoption service)
- Credentialing program + simulation lab integration
- Price: `$2,500-$5,000` per cohort

## Customer vs User

- User: Surgeon / Scrub Tech / Radiologist (operates gesture interface intraoperatively)
- Customer: Hospital System / IDN (procurement through capital budget or OR operations)
- Clinical Champion: Chief of Surgery / CMIO

## Market Context from Slides

- TAM: `~$40B-$50B` (global medical imaging + surgical tech market)
- SAM: `~$10B-$15B` (developed systems with real-time imaging OR workflows)
- SOM: `~$200M-$500M` in `3-5 years` (U.S. early adopters, teaching and innovation-driven hospitals)

## Modeling Assumptions

- Year 1: `3` hospitals x `4` OR suites (`12` units)
- Year 2: `8` hospitals x `6` OR suites (`48` units)
- Year 3: `20` hospitals x `8` OR suites (`160` units)
- Training cohorts assumed at one per hospital per year

## Revenue Projection (Low-High Range)

### Year 1

- Hardware: `12 x $18,000-$25,000 = $216,000-$300,000`
- SaaS: `12 x $8,400 = $100,800`
- Training: `3 x $2,500-$5,000 = $7,500-$15,000`
- Total: `$324,300-$415,800`

### Year 2

- Hardware: `48 x $18,000-$25,000 = $864,000-$1,200,000`
- SaaS: `48 x $8,400 = $403,200`
- Training: `8 x $2,500-$5,000 = $20,000-$40,000`
- Total: `$1,287,200-$1,643,200`

### Year 3

- Hardware: `160 x $18,000-$25,000 = $2,880,000-$4,000,000`
- SaaS: `160 x $8,400 = $1,344,000`
- Training: `20 x $2,500-$5,000 = $50,000-$100,000`
- Total: `$4,274,000-$5,444,000`

## Positioning

- Capital + recurring mix designed for hospital procurement cycles
- Price narrative emphasizes major cost advantage versus robotic systems approaching `$1M`
